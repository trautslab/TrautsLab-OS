=== 1. EXECUTIVE CONTEXT & DOMAIN SCOPE ===
The squad is responsible for the transactional backbone of retail banking, serving over 12 million active accounts with peak loads exceeding 4,500 TPS. The architecture has transitioned from an IBM Mainframe (z/OS DB2 CICS) monolith into an Event-Driven Architecture (EDA) on AWS, strictly aligned with BIAN v10 standards across 11 Service Domains:
- Domain 1: Current Account (Gestión de Cuentas de Ahorros y Corrientes)
- Domain 2: Payment Execution (Rieles Transaccionales, CCE, Yape/Plin, Pagos Inmediatos)
- Domain 3: Party Routing Profile (Directorio de Enrutamiento de Clientes y Alias)
- Domain 4: Customer Offer & Onboarding (Originación Digital de Cuentas y Biometría)
- Domain 5: Customer Agreement & Contract (Contratos Digitales, Términos Legales)
- Domain 6: Position Keeping & Balances (Motor de Saldos y Movimientos en Tiempo Real)
- Domain 7: Financial Accounting & General Ledger (Contabilidad Mayor y Asientos Diarios)
- Domain 8: Collateral Allocation & Guarantees (Garantías Líquidas y Líneas Asociadas)
- Domain 9: Fraud Detection & Risk Scoring (Motor Antifraude en Tiempo Real y Reglas)
- Domain 10: Customer Alert & Notification (Notificaciones Push, SMS, Email Transaccional)
- Domain 11: Audit, Logging & Regulatory Compliance (Trazabilidad Regulatoria SBS y UIF)

=== 3. BACKBONE EVENT BUSES & ORCHESTRATION INFRASTRUCTURE ===
All domains are interconnected via an Amazon EventBridge Enterprise Bus (`arn:aws:events:us-east-1:123456789012:event-bus/core-banking-eventbus`):
- Key Event Types:
  * `AccountOpenedEvent.v1`, `AccountStateChangedEvent.v1`, `BalanceReservedEvent.v1`, `BalanceCommittedEvent.v1`
  * `PaymentInitiatedEvent.v1`, `PaymentAuthorizedEvent.v1`, `PaymentCompletedEvent.v1`, `PaymentFailedEvent.v1`
  * `FraudRiskScoreCalculatedEvent.v1`, `AccountFrozenEvent.v1`, `LedgerVoucherPostedEvent.v1`
  * `NotificationDispatchedEvent.v1`, `ComplianceReportGeneratedEvent.v1`

Dead-Letter Queues (DLQs) are configured on Amazon SQS for every EventBridge Rule with maximum 3 retry attempts before dispatching to CloudWatch Alarms.
Long-running distributed transactions are orchestrated via AWS Step Functions Express & Standard State Machines with automated backward compensation.
